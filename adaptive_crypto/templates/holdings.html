How momentum alerts work{% if s.is_smc %}

A completed {{ s.entry_minutes }}M close above the latest confirmed swing high sets bullish direction; a close below the latest confirmed swing low sets bearish direction. Direction persists until an opposing structure break. Swings require {{ s.rules.smc_pivot_strength }} completed candles on each side. Wicks and equality do not change direction. Direction is neutral until the first break. Entry still requires the full sweep/order-block/gap sequence.

{% else %}

Bullish: ROC({{ s.rules.momentum_roc_period }}) is above zero and above its EMA({{ s.rules.momentum_signal_period }}) signal. Bearish: ROC is below zero and below its signal. Otherwise momentum is neutral.

{% endif %}

The opening reading establishes a baseline. A later change into bullish or bearish momentum creates one alert per open position and candle. {% if s.rules.market_mode == "spot" %}Bearish momentum signals SELL TO EXIT LONG for a spot holding; bullish momentum says HOLD LONG.{% else %}Bearish momentum signals SELL TO EXIT LONG; bullish momentum signals BUY TO COVER SHORT for an existing margin short sale.{% endif %} A supporting change says HOLD. Alerts include the entry price and a fresh bid/ask exit quote, or an explicitly labeled candle reference. Neutral changes do not send an alert. Formula or timeframe changes establish a new quiet baseline.

Entry price and quantity determine gross P/L. {% if s.rules.market_mode == "spot" %}Spot gross P/L = (sale or current bid price − purchase price) × quantity. A sale below the purchase price is a loss.{% else %}Long P/L rises with price; margin short P/L rises when price falls.{% endif %} P/L excludes fees and funding. This records your holdings and does not submit exchange orders or use the paper balance.
