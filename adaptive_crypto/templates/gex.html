    Model and coverage

    Calls contribute positive gamma; puts negative. Walls are the largest positive and negative net GEX strikes across active expiries. Gamma is estimated from mark IV using zero-rate Black–Scholes. GEX = signed gamma × open interest in base coins × index price² × 1%. Units: USD delta per 1% move (USDC for SOL).

    The flip reprices the chain with fixed IV and open interest over ±30% of the index. The nearest detected sign change is shown; a flip may be absent or multiple. This is a numerical estimate, not a forecast.
