# Selectable NN candidate evaluation (28 September 2026)

**Result: no demonstrated winner.** Four genuinely trained per-asset classifiers and a fixed, equal-weight probability ensemble are technically valid research choices, but none satisfies the predeclared replacement gate against bundled Parente on its supported BTC/ETH/SOL interval. The current model is not changed automatically. The grouped attention-LSTM is an **OHLCV classifier adaptation**; it is not a reproduction of the on-chain regression SAM-LSTM study.

## Controlled experiment

The source is Kraken spot **USD** completed UTC 4H OHLCV: the official full 2026Q2 OHLCVT archive plus a verified, committed Kraken REST tail. The archived 240-minute ZIP members were fetched by byte ranges, checked by ZIP CRC, normalized and SHA-256 hashed. The published whole-archive checksum was **not** independently verified because the full archive is about 9 GB. The REST tail overlaps the archive on each pair, checks OHLCV equality and excludes the unfinished response bar. Exact input and retrieval hashes are in `.qa/candidate_data/kraken_2026q2_plus_rest/provenance.json`; the normalized combined test source is `.qa/candidate_runs/final_v1/combined_candles_4h.csv` (SHA-256 `01e20e4c3bc1caef9acd367d39db3e9efe522e163b45b5f36ba85ce1a3e2287d`). The local `.qa` directory is an experiment record, not a deployable model directory. Retain its hashed inputs for bit-for-bit replay; a later live REST response can contain revised or newer data.

BTC, ETH, SOL and SUI each have independently trained model members. Gaps are recorded and sequences crossing them are dropped; no synthetic candles are filled. All four networks use the same causal 16-feature schema, fixed 256-raw-bar feature context, 64 feature rows and 319 contiguous completed 4H bars at inference. Scalers are fitted on training samples only. Targets use source-convention Parente 5/2 labels with a two-bar forward horizon; unknown tails and boundary-crossing targets are purged. Class order is `BUY, HOLD, SELL`. The training window starts 2023-05-03 12:00 UTC; validation starts 2025-09-19 20:00 UTC; final test runs 2026-01-22 04:00 through 2026-09-27 20:00 UTC (exclusive end). Two earlier expanding folds and three fixed seeds per architecture/asset support validation; the final test was held out for confirmation. The frozen protocol is `experiment.json` in the experiment directory.

Each per-asset member's weight-training cutoff is **2025-09-19 19:59:59.999 UTC**. Validation determines its seed/epoch before the 2026-01-22 test boundary. The manifests conservatively set the model's **live-eligibility cutoff to 2026-09-27 19:59:59.999 UTC** because final-test technical promotion is part of the artifact evidence. These are different dates; the later eligibility cutoff is not a claim that gradients were fit on the test interval.

The final four-asset test has **5,960 complete signal/target pairs**. Parente has no bundled SUI calibration and needs 720 contiguous completed bars. An earlier test gap therefore moves its common BTC/ETH/SOL comparison start to **2026-03-01 16:00 UTC**; the end remains 2026-09-27 20:00 UTC. This coverage-only boundary was frozen without examining predictions or performance. The common evaluation has **3,777** pairs, identical market data, target audit, costs, paper sizing policy and mark cadence for every model. This comparison does not imply that Parente's historical training distribution matches the new training distribution.

The evaluator uses the earliest next 4H open after each signal as a midpoint **execution proxy**, applying a 10 bps synthetic spread, 0.4% fee per side and 0.05% slippage per side. Higher-cost stress doubles those three assumptions. It replays limitations both ON and OFF, with the existing paper cash, sizing, stop and asset-order semantics. The table uses **OFF**, matching the observed applied setting at experiment start. Open lots are marked to net-liquidation equity every 4H; remaining lots receive one evaluation-only terminal liquidation, excluded from natural-exit counts. For limited-mode trades, the simulated entry bar's low can confirm a stop at the next observation because the proxy fills exactly at its open; the stop trigger and gap fill use synthetic bids. A live quote can arrive later than that open. Intrabar stop timing and actual historical bid/ask are unavailable without a complete verified 5M/bid-ask archive; the 4H proxy cannot establish an exact real fill or whether an earlier intrabar exit was missed. The cash/no-trade base return is 0%; equal initial-cash buy-and-hold on the same three assets is +33.74% after the same entry/terminal cost assumptions. The high accuracy of several classifiers is mostly the `HOLD` majority (94.63% on the common interval), which is why accuracy alone is not a recommendation. The simple controls use the exact causal final 16-feature row and split boundaries. Their trained logistic scalers and weights, training class counts, prediction hashes and independent after-cost reports are in `.qa/candidate_runs/final_v1/simple_baselines/`. They are research controls, not selectable models.

The gate in `tools/compare_nn_candidate_reports.py` requires complete artifact/label/coverage checks, at least 30 **natural** out-of-sample exits, positive base and doubled-cost returns, better paired net return than Parente, no worse sampled drawdown, and a positive lower 95% moving-block paired improvement bound. All new candidates fail the 30-exit floor (the ensemble's apparent gain includes a final forced liquidation with **zero** natural exits). GRU and LSTM also exceed Parente's drawdown; attention-LSTM is a no-trade result. The gate reports **no demonstrated winner** in `.qa/candidate_runs/final_v1/candidate_comparison.final.json`. This gate does not automatically change the selected model or the limitations checkbox. Two independent expanding folds across four assets generated 5,952 outer validation observations per architecture: mean macro-F1 was 0.376 (LSTM), 0.394 (GRU), 0.395 (CNN-LSTM) and 0.307 (attention-LSTM). This dispersion also gives no clear basis for replacing Parente.

## Trained artifacts and runtime validation

The four immutable model IDs are `lstm_classifier_v1@4033451e9329`, `gru_classifier_v1@1f83df264a1e`, `cnn_lstm_classifier_v1@79f9860590eb`, and `grouped_attention_lstm_v1@2e29d734b5b6`. Each bundle in `adaptive_crypto/models/candidates/<family>/<version>/` has real independently fitted per-asset ONNX weights, train-only scalers, data/split/seed evidence, a manifest and a hashed report. The equal-weight ensemble bundles four verified member manifest hashes; its final ID is `probability_ensemble_v1@3801b1dabe20` (full artifact SHA-256 `3801b1dabe200b4c4b24182f9b566ecb0aedb45ad505bf93b38b47e8ebd26795`). Parente remains the original strict NPZ loader and is available only for BTC, ETH and SOL. A selector shows unsupported assets as unavailable rather than substituting a different model.

Training ran in an isolated Windows CPython 3.12.14 environment with PyTorch `2.14.0+cpu`, ONNX `1.23.0`, ONNX Script `0.7.2`, NumPy `2.5.3` and Requests `2.34.2`. Runtime ONNX Runtime CPU `1.30.0` uses opset 17. Frozen backtest versus live-adapter export parity checked 12 held-out signals per architecture across four assets; each maximum probability difference was below `7e-8` against a `1e-5` tolerance. The final ensemble separately matched 12 frozen signals with a maximum difference of `1.78e-8`.

A fresh Windows process per architecture measured 50 sequential four-asset predictions on prepared, complete 319-bar inputs. This **full adapter** timing includes feature construction, scaling, ONNX CPU inference and softmax, but excludes data fetching and dashboard scheduling. Four-asset p50/p95 times were LSTM **31.57/36.85 ms**, GRU **31.30/33.34 ms**, CNN-LSTM **31.37/32.37 ms** and grouped attention-LSTM **31.52/35.92 ms**. Warm process working sets after model load were **60.7–62.4 MB** (about 38–40 MB above each process's pre-load working set). The results are workstation observations, not a guarantee for a different host; exact measurements are in each bundle report and `.qa/candidate_runs/final_v1/runtime_benchmark.json`.

## Reproduce the offline evidence

The existing frozen input paths below permit a deterministic replay without network access or changing live Settings. Run from the repository root. Use fresh output filenames because the commands refuse to overwrite immutable reports.

```powershell
$nnRun = '.qa/candidate_runs/final_v1'
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m tools.evaluate_nn_candidates --predictions "$nnRun/lstm_classifier_v1.4033451e9329.predictions.jsonl" --candles-4h "$nnRun/combined_candles_4h.csv" --experiment "$nnRun/comparable_experiment.json" --verify-source-labels --scope-predictions --output "$nnRun/recheck-lstm-comparable.json"
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m tools.train_nn_candidates verify-runtime --output $nnRun
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m tools.verify_nn_ensemble_runtime --bundle adaptive_crypto/models/candidates/probability_ensemble_v1/3801b1dabe20 --predictions "$nnRun/probability_ensemble_v1.final.predictions.jsonl" --candles-4h "$nnRun/combined_candles_4h.csv" --output "$nnRun/recheck-ensemble-runtime.json"
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m unittest test_candidate_evaluation test_nn_simple_baselines -q
```

To reacquire and retrain, use `tools.train_nn_candidates archive-fetch`, then `rest-merge`, then `train --data <verified-directory> --output <new-run-directory>`, followed by `train-folds`, `attach-folds` and `verify-runtime`. Use the pinned isolated environment in `requirements-candidate-training.txt`. The REST snapshot and hence artifact hashes can differ on a later date; compare each new run with its own frozen input/provenance hashes. `tools.train_nn_simple_baselines` fits the two simple controls on the same data and requires a frozen candidate prediction grid as a target/coverage cross-check. The complete frozen reports, per-asset metrics, confusion matrices, calibration bins, fees, turnover, ON/OFF and stress replays are in the experiment directory and each bundle's `report.json`.

The optional social/on-chain branches were not trained: no historical as-of dataset was verified. Current AI market summaries and retrospectively found posts are not used as historical features.
